# Wild & Well — Version 1 Baseline

**Baseline date:** 28 August 2026

This document marks the end of the Version 1 rebuild. Future structural changes should be driven by evidence from search visibility, user behaviour, affiliate clicks, conversion data or a clear editorial/business requirement rather than continued redesign for its own sake.

## Positioning

Wild & Well is a UK-focused, education-first wellness, healthy-home and practical-resilience site. The editorial pattern is:

**education → no-spend steps → focused buyer route → maintained shortlist → approved partner option**

The commercial architecture follows the rule:

**many useful SEO entry points → few strong commercial hubs**

Affiliate relationships do not determine recommendations. Commercial pages should explain trade-offs, disclose affiliate relationships and avoid unnecessary purchases, exaggerated health claims or fear-led preparedness framing.

## Seven primary commercial journeys

| Journey | Learn / discovery | Primary commercial destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water | `/topics/water` | `/water-filtration-shortlist-uk` |
| Air | `/topics/air-quality` | `/air-quality-shortlist-uk` |
| Sleep | `/topics/sleep` | `/sleep-recovery-shortlist-uk` |
| Nutrition | `/nutrition` | `/nutrition/food-first-shortlist` |
| Movement | `/movement` | `/movement/movement-shortlist` |
| Healthy Home | `/healthy-home` | `/healthy-home/low-tox-shortlist` |
| Resilience | `/topics/resilience` | `/blog/72-hour-household-emergency-kit-uk` |

Long-tail buyer pages may remain for search intent, but they should route visitors toward these maintained destinations rather than develop independent duplicate product catalogues.

## Version 1 QA gate

Every push to `main` and every pull request must pass:

1. `npm ci`
2. `npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high`
3. `npm run check:frontmatter`
4. `npm run check:links`
5. `npm run build`

At handoff, the verified QA baseline is:

- **0 npm audit vulnerabilities**
- **101 content files** passing frontmatter validation
- **225 static routes** passing the internal-link checker
- **231 pages/routes** generated successfully by the production build

## SEO and indexability baseline

- `robots.txt` allows public crawling and points to the sitemap.
- `sitemap.xml` discovers static app routes and published blog content while excluding known legacy commercial routes.
- Legacy URLs are retained through permanent redirects where appropriate.
- Article pages include Article and BreadcrumbList structured data.
- Important hubs have descriptive page metadata.
- Affiliate links generated by shared commercial components use sponsored/nofollow handling and contextual click references where available.

## Commercial / editorial baseline

- Central shortlists carry visible affiliate disclosure language.
- Product cards use maintained Awin destinations where approved links exist.
- Food-first nutrition remains the primary nutrition buying philosophy; supplements are secondary.
- Healthy Home prioritises gradual high-use swaps instead of wholesale replacement.
- Resilience prioritises ordinary short-term disruption, stored essentials and practical household planning before specialist equipment.
- Movement prioritises repeatable activity before gadgets.
- Sleep pages prioritise sleep basics and the actual problem before products.
- Air pages distinguish particle filtration, humidity control and ventilation.
- Water pages distinguish stored water, filtration formats, running costs and hard-water comfort.

## PlayGlide boundary

**PlayGlide by Wild & Well** remains a future kids' movement concept only. It is not presented as a purchasable product and no performance claims should be made until prototyping and real-world testing justify them.

## Architecture freeze

Version 1 architecture is now considered closed. The next phase is **traffic, indexing and monetisation**.

Priorities after this baseline are:

- Google Search Console indexing and query/impression analysis;
- SEO work based on real search data;
- content distribution and social discovery;
- email-list growth;
- affiliate click and conversion measurement;
- improving pages with actual impressions but weak click-through or conversion;
- expanding approved affiliate options only where they strengthen an existing journey;
- PlayGlide research/prototyping as a separate owned-product workstream.

A future architecture change should have a measurable reason. Do not reopen the rebuild simply because another layout or site structure is possible.
