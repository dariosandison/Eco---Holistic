If you dislike the taste of your tap water or simply prefer filtered drinking water, the choice often becomes **home filtration or buying bottled water regularly**.

There is no universal answer, but the long-term cost and convenience can look very different.

## Bottled water is simple but repetitive

The main advantage is obvious: no installation, no cartridges and no maintenance beyond buying more bottles.

The drawbacks are repeated purchasing, carrying and storing bottles, plus the amount of packaging involved. For a household using several litres a day, the recurring cost can add up quickly.

## Home filtration shifts the cost upfront

A jug, countertop, gravity or under-sink system usually means buying equipment and replacement filters rather than paying for each bottle of water.

The right comparison is therefore not just purchase price. Estimate **annual filter cost** and compare it with what you realistically spend on bottled water over the same period.

See **[Water Filter Replacement Costs UK](/blog/filter-replacement-costs)** before making that calculation.

## Convenience depends on format

A jug has the lowest barrier to entry but needs frequent refilling. A countertop or gravity filter offers more capacity but occupies space. An under-sink system is convenient once installed but costs more and requires plumbing and cartridge maintenance.

Compare those routes in **[Water Filter Jug vs Under-Sink Filter UK](/blog/water-filter-jug-vs-under-sink-uk)**.

## Do not assume bottled automatically means 'better'

If your reason for avoiding tap water is a specific water-quality concern, identify that concern rather than treating all bottled water as one product category and all tap water as another.

Likewise, do not buy an elaborate filtration system unless its stated reduction performance is relevant to what you actually want to change.

## A simple decision rule

Choose bottled water when occasional use and zero maintenance matter most.

Consider a home filter when you use filtered water regularly and are willing to replace cartridges on schedule.
