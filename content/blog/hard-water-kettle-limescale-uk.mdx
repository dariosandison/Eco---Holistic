If your kettle quickly develops a white, chalky crust, hard water is a likely reason. In many UK areas, mains water contains relatively high levels of calcium and magnesium. When water is heated, scale can build up on the kettle element and inner surfaces.

## Does a drinking-water filter stop kettle limescale?

Not necessarily. This is where buying the wrong product is easy.

Many drinking-water filters are designed primarily around taste, odour or specific contaminants. A filter can be useful for those goals without meaningfully solving hardness minerals.

If your only complaint is **limescale**, check whether the product specifically addresses hardness rather than assuming every filter does.

## The three different problems people often mix together

### 1. Kettle scale
This is mainly a maintenance and hardness issue. Regular descaling is the simplest immediate fix.

### 2. Drinking-water taste
If you dislike the taste or smell of your tap water, a drinking-water filter may be relevant even if it does not soften the water.

### 3. Scale throughout the home
If shower screens, taps, appliances and plumbing all suffer from heavy scale, the more relevant conversation is usually **whole-home water softening**, not a small drinking-water filter.

## How to reduce kettle scale without buying a new filtration system

- descale the kettle regularly according to the manufacturer's instructions;
- avoid repeatedly boiling more water than you need;
- empty standing water if that suits the kettle manufacturer's guidance;
- wipe loose scale after descaling rather than allowing it to accumulate.

## Filter jug, under-sink filter or softener?

A **filter jug** can be useful when you want a simple drinking-water change with no installation.

An **under-sink filter** can make sense when you want filtered drinking water at the tap and prefer higher capacity.

A **water softener** is the more relevant category when the main objective is reducing hardness effects throughout the home.

A shower filter and a water softener are not interchangeable. If your concern is shower-water filtration rather than whole-home scale, see **[Shower Filters for UK Hard Water: what they can and can't do](/best-shower-filters-uk-hard-water)**.

## Quick decision rule

**Only the kettle scales up?** Descale it first.

**You dislike drinking-water taste?** Compare drinking-water filtration.

**The whole home suffers from scale?** Investigate water softening rather than assuming a jug or shower filter will solve it.
