The purchase price is only half the decision. For filters, purifiers and shower systems, the **replacement schedule is the real ownership cost**.

> **Quick routes:** [Water filtration shortlist →](/water-filtration-shortlist-uk) • [Air quality shortlist →](/air-quality-shortlist-uk) • [Shower filters →](/best-shower-filters-uk-hard-water)

## The 30-second rule

Before buying any filter-based product, answer three questions:

1. **What exact replacement part does it use?**
2. **How often will you realistically replace it?**
3. **What will that cost over 12 months?**

The simple formula is:

**Annual replacement cost = replacement price × replacements per year**

Then add the original purchase price if you want a realistic first-year figure.

## Why this changes the buying decision

A cheaper device is not always the cheaper option. A slightly more expensive unit can cost less over time if its filters last longer, are sold in multipacks, or are easier to source in the UK.

The opposite is also true: a low headline price can become poor value if replacement cartridges are expensive or frequently unavailable.

<Note>
Do not rely only on the manufacturer’s longest quoted lifespan. Heavy use, pets, smoke, hard water, high particle loads and large households can all shorten replacement intervals.
</Note>

## Water filter costs

### Jugs and countertop filters
Check:
- price per cartridge, not just pack price
- litres or months per cartridge
- how many litres your household is likely to use
- whether genuine replacements are easy to find

For a maintained comparison, go to the [Water filtration shortlist →](/water-filtration-shortlist-uk#jugs-dispensers).

### Under-sink and reverse-osmosis systems
These can have several replacement stages, each with a different service interval. Price the **whole replacement cycle**, not just the main cartridge.

Check:
- sediment or pre-filter cost
- carbon-stage cost
- membrane cost where applicable
- installation or servicing requirements

Compare the current routes here: [Under-sink & RO shortlist →](/water-filtration-shortlist-uk#under-sink-ro).

### Gravity systems
The key numbers are element lifespan, replacement-element cost and whether replacement parts remain readily available.

## Shower filter costs

A shower filter should be treated as a specific shower-water filtration purchase, not as a whole-home water-softening solution.

Before buying, check:
- cartridge price
- expected replacement interval
- compatibility with your shower setup
- whether the stated filtration claims match your actual reason for buying

Use the dedicated guide before committing: [Best shower filters for UK hard water →](/best-shower-filters-uk-hard-water).

## Air purifier costs

The filter can become the biggest long-term cost of owning an air purifier.

Check:
- whether HEPA and carbon are separate or combined
- official replacement price
- expected lifespan at your likely run time
- UK stock availability
- whether you are likely to run the purifier all day or only at certain times

## A practical first-year comparison

When two products look similar, compare them like this:

| Cost | Option A | Option B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Device price | £___ | £___ |
| Replacement filters in year 1 | £___ | £___ |
| Other required parts | £___ | £___ |
| **First-year total** | **£___** | **£___** |

Then ask one final question: **which replacement schedule am I actually likely to maintain?**

A technically excellent filter that you stop replacing is a poor purchase.

## Three buyer rules worth keeping

1. **If the replacement is hard to find before you buy, assume it may be harder later.**
2. **If the annual replacement cost feels uncomfortable now, do not ignore it.**
3. **Buy for the problem you actually have, not for the longest feature list.**
