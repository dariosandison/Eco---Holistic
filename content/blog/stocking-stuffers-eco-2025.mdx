This 2025 seasonal page is archived and is no longer maintained as a current recommendation guide.

The useful principle remains simple: choose small gifts that are likely to be used rather than novelty products bought only to fill space.

Examples include consumables the recipient already likes, practical repair items, durable clothing basics and genuinely useful reusable items.
