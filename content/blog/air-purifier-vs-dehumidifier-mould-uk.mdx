Air purifiers and dehumidifiers solve different problems, yet they are often marketed beside each other. If you are dealing with mould or damp, choosing the wrong category can waste money.

## The simplest distinction

A **dehumidifier removes moisture from the air**.

An **air purifier filters particles from the air**.

If excess humidity is allowing condensation and mould to keep returning, reducing moisture is usually the more relevant first step. If humidity is already controlled but you want to reduce airborne particles, a purifier may be more relevant.

## When a dehumidifier makes more sense

A dehumidifier is the stronger fit when you have signs such as:

- repeated condensation on windows;
- persistently high indoor humidity;
- laundry taking a long time to dry indoors;
- a room that feels damp;
- mould returning where moisture remains a problem.

Before buying, still check for leaks, poor extraction, blocked vents or other building issues. A dehumidifier manages moisture; it does not repair the source of water ingress.

## When an air purifier makes more sense

An air purifier may be useful when your priority is airborne particles such as dust, pollen or smoke and the room's humidity is already in a reasonable range.

For bedrooms and small flats, room coverage, useful-speed noise and replacement-filter cost usually matter more than app features.

See **[Air Purifiers for Small Flats UK](/best-air-purifiers-small-flats-uk)** or the **[allergy-focused purifier guide](/best-air-purifiers-allergies-uk)**.

## Can you use both?

Yes, but only when you genuinely have both problems. For example, a damp room with excessive humidity may need moisture control first, while a purifier could later be used for particle filtration.

Buying both immediately without measuring the problem usually makes less sense than identifying the main issue first.

## What if there is visible mould?

The priority is to address the moisture source and clean or remediate the affected area appropriately. Do not rely on a purifier to make an unresolved damp problem disappear.

## Quick comparison

| Problem | More relevant starting point |
| --- | --- |
| Condensation | Dehumidifier / ventilation |
| High humidity | Dehumidifier |
| Dust or pollen | Air purifier |
| Smoke particles | Air purifier |
| Leak or penetrating damp | Repair the source |
| Mould plus high humidity | Moisture control first |
