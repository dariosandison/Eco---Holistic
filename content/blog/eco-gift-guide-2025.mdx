This 2025 seasonal guide is archived and no longer maintained as a current Wild & Well recommendation page.

## Evergreen principles

- Prefer experiences when they genuinely suit the recipient.
- Consumables can reduce long-term clutter when they are things the person already enjoys.
- Choose durable tools for a known use rather than novelty gadgets.
- Reuse boxes, bags and wrapping materials where practical.
