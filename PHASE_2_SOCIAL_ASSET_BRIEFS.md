# Wild & Well — Social Creative Briefs

Use with `PHASE_2_LAUNCH_PACK.md`. These are production-ready creative instructions for Canva, Adobe Express or a native social editor. Keep the existing Wild & Well visual identity; do not introduce a second brand system.

## Static post / carousel template

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5).  
**Safe area:** keep important text at least 80 px from edges.  
**Style:** warm natural photography or simple clean illustration, off-white/neutral space, dark readable type, restrained accent colour from the site.  
**Brand footer:** `WILD & WELL  •  wild-and-well.store` in small type.  
**Rule:** one idea per slide; avoid tiny paragraphs.

## Pinterest template

**Canvas:** 1000 × 1500 px (2:3).  
**Top:** short problem-led headline.  
**Middle:** relevant home/wellness image or simple iconography.  
**Bottom:** `Practical UK guide • Wild & Well` and `wild-and-well.store`.  
Do not put prices on graphics because prices change.

## Reel / TikTok / Short template

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 px (9:16).  
**Length:** normally 15–30 seconds.  
**First 2 seconds:** display the exact hook from the launch pack in large text.  
**Body:** 3–5 quick scenes/cards, each no more than about 8 words on screen.  
**End card:** `Learn first. Buy second. • Wild & Well` plus `wild-and-well.store`.  
**Audio:** clear spoken voiceover; music optional and low enough not to compete with speech.  
**Captions:** always enable burned-in/native captions.

## Where to paste / upload

### Instagram feed
1. Instagram → `+` → **Post** (or Meta Business Suite → Create post → Instagram).
2. Upload the 1080×1350 graphic(s).
3. Paste the matching **Instagram caption** from `PHASE_2_LAUNCH_PACK.md` into **Write a caption**.
4. If the post says `link in bio`, set the relevant Wild & Well destination in **Edit profile → Links** before publishing, or keep the main Wild & Well homepage link and tell users to use the named section.
5. Add alt text in Advanced settings when available; describe the image plainly rather than keyword stuffing.

### Facebook Page
1. Meta Business Suite → **Create post** → select the Wild & Well Facebook Page.
2. Upload the same 1080×1350 asset (or use 1200×1500 if rebuilding specifically for Facebook).
3. Paste the matching Facebook caption into **Post details**.
4. Keep the full Wild & Well destination URL included in the post where supplied.
5. Schedule/publish.

### Pinterest
1. Pinterest business account → **Create Pin**.
2. Upload the 1000×1500 graphic.
3. Paste **Pin title** into Title.
4. Paste **Description** into Description.
5. Paste the full `https://www.wild-and-well.store/...` URL into **Link**.
6. Select the board named in the launch pack. If it does not exist, create that board using the exact suggested name.
7. Publish or schedule.

### Instagram Reel
1. Instagram → `+` → **Reel**.
2. Record/create the scenes from the relevant script in `PHASE_2_LAUNCH_PACK.md`.
3. Put the **Hook** on the opening frame.
4. Use the **Script** as voiceover and enable captions.
5. Paste the supplied **Caption** into the caption field.
6. Use a simple cover frame containing the hook; avoid dense text.

### TikTok
1. TikTok → `+` → upload/record 9:16 video.
2. Use the same hook/script as the Reel.
3. Enable captions and correct obvious transcription errors.
4. Paste the supplied short-form caption.
5. Send people to Wild & Well through the profile link; do not paste raw affiliate links into the post.

### YouTube Shorts
1. YouTube Studio → **Create → Upload videos**.
2. Upload the vertical video.
3. Use the hook as the first part of the title; keep title natural rather than stuffing hashtags.
4. Put the relevant full Wild & Well article URL in the description with one sentence explaining what it contains.
5. Mark audience accurately; these Wild & Well videos are general wellness/home information, not content made specifically for children.

## Asset workflow

Batch-create the 10 hero graphics first. Reuse their layout language across the month while changing imagery/headline. The goal is recognition and speed, not 30 completely different designs. For carousel days, duplicate the hero template and use the exact slide text from the launch pack.
